Worked example — thermometer cup

Physical product · the three beats above

Brief: Reusable mug with an exterior temperature readout so you know drink heat without tasting. Not a smart app. Not medical device claims.

Done-when: Parts ordered and one assembled prototype with a readable exterior temp — or a quote for a factory mug with a printed scale.

BOM (existing catalogs):

  • Blank stainless tumbler / mug — Amazon / restaurant supply
  • External stick-on LCD thermometer strip or mini digital probe panel
  • Food-safe adhesive / mounting band — McMaster or Amazon
  • Optional: sleeve / silicone grip to hold the readout

Build path: Buy the blank. Buy the exterior readout. Mount below the lip. Calibrate with hot water and a kitchen thermometer. If the strip fails, swap to a discrete digital panel.

Prototype order: Tumbler + LCD strip on Amazon today; assemble tonight. Next: factory blank + printed scale, or a local shop for a clip-on housing.
