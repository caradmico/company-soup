The Salty Heifer Diner

Cara Mico · Ledyard, CT · August 8, 2026

Open on Maps

Incredible farm to table without the pretense. Great prices, delicious perfectly cooked food. Had the steak and eggs — best steak I’ve had on the East Coast, fresh from their farm. Had the local coconut ice cream, amazing. Eggs were perfect.

Farm to table without the pretense. That is a rare sentence that still means something. The food is delicious and perfectly cooked, and the prices are great — a diner doing the work, not a lecture about the farm.

I had the steak and eggs. Best steak I’ve had on the East Coast, fresh from their farm. I am not hedging that. The plate was the plate: steak, eggs, cooked right. The eggs were perfect.

Then the local coconut ice cream. Amazing. That is the dessert note, and it belongs next to the steak.

Ledyard. Colonel Ledyard Highway. Go for that steak.
