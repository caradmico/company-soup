Review · Ledyard, CT

The Salty Heifer Diner

Cara Mico · August 8, 2026

Open on Maps

Incredible farm to table without the pretense. Great prices, delicious perfectly cooked food. Had the steak and eggs — best steak I’ve had on the East Coast.

Farm to table without the pretense. That is a rare sentence that still means something. The food is delicious and perfectly cooked, and the prices are great — a diner doing the work, not a lecture about the farm.

I had the steak and eggs. Best steak I’ve had on the East Coast. I am not hedging that. The plate was the plate: steak, eggs, cooked right.

Ledyard. Go for that steak.
