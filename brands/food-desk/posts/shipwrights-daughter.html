Review · Mystic, CT

The Shipwright’s Daughter

Cara Mico · around September 7, 2026

Open on Maps

A vibrant gem, includes perfectly prepared local seafood and local farm ingredients.

A vibrant gem. Mystic has rooms that try too hard. This one is alive without the costume.

The seafood is local and perfectly prepared. The farm ingredients are local too. That pairing is the whole promise of a coastal kitchen — water and field on the same plate, cooked like both mattered.

East Main. Sit down for the seafood.
