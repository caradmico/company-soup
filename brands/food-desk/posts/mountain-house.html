The Mountain House

Cara Mico · 2025

Open on Maps

A delightful restaurant in one of the most beautiful places in the world, they have a new happy hour Wednesdays and Thursdays.

A delightful restaurant in one of the most beautiful places in the world. Skyline Boulevard, Woodside — the drive is part of the meal, and the room does not waste the view.

They have a new happy hour Wednesdays and Thursdays. That is the practical sentence. Delightful food in a place that already looks like a reason to go, and a midweek hour that makes sitting down easier.

Go up the mountain on a Wednesday or a Thursday if you can.
