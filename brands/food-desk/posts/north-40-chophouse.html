North 40 Chophouse

Cara Mico · North Platte, NE · July 28, 2026

Open on Maps

Delicious. Local beef, butchered locally as well. Wonderful dry-aged cuts.

The beef is local, and they butcher it locally too. That is the whole point of a chophouse worth sitting down in — the animal did not travel farther than the plate.

The dry-aged cuts are wonderful. You taste the age and the meat, not a sauce doing the work. Delicious is the right word because the steak does not need a longer one.

North Platte. Local beef. Dry-aged. That is the review.
