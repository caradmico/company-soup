Patio Coffee Shop

Cara Mico · March 8, 2025

Open on Maps

Lovely simple breakfast and lunch cafe, good Americano and nice options at an affordable price, no wait, fast service, kind people.

A lovely, simple breakfast and lunch cafe. That is the job, and they do it. Nice options at an affordable price — you can eat here without making a project of it.

The Americano is good. No wait. Fast service. Kind people. I keep those four in a row because that is how the morning felt: coffee, then food, then out the door, treated well.

San Mateo. The simple cafe you actually want.
