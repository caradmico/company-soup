A quiet hole in the wall with exceptional authentic Italian, generous portions.

A quiet hole in the wall. That is a compliment. You find it on Thames Street in Groton and the room does not need to perform.

The Italian is exceptional and authentic. The portions are generous. Pasta cooked like someone still cares about the water and the sauce.

Groton. Quiet. Generous. Go hungry.
