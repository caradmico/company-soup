Free · public lookup · starter list

Grants by type

A short list of free public databases and program pages you can open today. This is a starter, not a catalog of every funder. We do not list invented awards or dollar amounts.

Each link is a lookup the host publishes for free. Read their current guidelines — cycles open and close.

Arts & culture

  • Grants.gov — Search Grants Free

    Federal opportunities, including arts-related notices. Filter by keyword, agency, and eligibility.

    https://www.grants.gov/search-grants

  • National Endowment for the Arts — Grants Free

    NEA program pages and current guidelines (Grants for Arts Projects and related categories).

    https://www.arts.gov/grants

  • Oregon Arts Commission — Grants Overview Free

    State arts grant programs for Oregon organizations and artists.

    https://www.oregon.gov/biz/aboutus/artscommission/grants/Pages/default.aspx

  • Oregon Cultural Trust — Grants Free

    Cultural Development grants and how Trust dollars move to partners and coalitions.

    https://www.culturaltrust.org/grants/

  • Regional Arts & Culture Council — Grants Free

    Portland-metro / tri-county arts grant programs and partner opportunities.

    https://racc.org/grants/

  • Oregon Humanities — Public Program Grants Free

    Public humanities program grants for Oregon organizations.

    https://oregonhumanities.org/programs/grants/public-program-grants-2/

  • Oregon Community Foundation — Grants (Arts & Culture filter) Free

    OCF’s public grants list; filter by Arts & Culture or region.

    https://oregoncf.org/grants-and-scholarships/grants

Community / nonprofit

  • Grants.gov Free

    Official U.S. federal grant search and application portal.

    https://www.grants.gov/

  • SAM.gov — Assistance Listings Free

    Official catalog of federal assistance programs (the listings formerly on CFDA).

    https://sam.gov/content/assistance-listings

  • Oregon Community Foundation — Grants Free

    Open OCF funds, including Community Grants. Filter by region and impact area.

    https://oregoncf.org/grants-and-scholarships/grants

  • Oregon Community Foundation — Grants Resources & FAQs Free

    How to find OCF funds and use the public application path.

    https://oregoncf.org/grants-and-scholarships/grants-resources

  • Meyer Memorial Trust — How We Fund Free

    Meyer’s current funding pathways and where they post open opportunities.

    https://mmt.org/our-grantmaking/how-we-fund/

  • U.S. Senator Ron Wyden — Grants Free

    Oregon-facing pointer page to Grants.gov, SAM.gov listings, and related federal lookup tools.

    https://www.wyden.senate.gov/services/grants

  • Candid Learning — Government grants Free

    Free explainer pointing to Grants.gov, SAM.gov assistance listings, and the Federal Register.

    https://learning.candid.org/government-grants/278275

Environment / watershed

  • Oregon Watershed Enhancement Board — Grant Programs Free

    OWEB’s public list of grant types: restoration, monitoring, technical assistance, and more.

    https://www.oregon.gov/oweb/grants/pages/grant-programs.aspx

  • OWEB — Restoration Grants Free

    Current restoration grant window, eligibility, and how to apply online.

    https://www.oregon.gov/oweb/grants/pages/restoration.aspx

  • OWEB — Coastal Wetlands Grants Free

    Oregon’s path into the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service National Coastal Wetlands Conservation Grant Program.

    https://www.oregon.gov/oweb/grants/pages/coastal-wetlands.aspx

  • Oregon Parks Foundation Fund (via OCF) Free

    Public page for park acquisition, habitat, and interpretive projects in Oregon parks.

    https://oregoncf.org/grants-and-scholarships/grants/oregon-parks-foundation-fund

  • EPA — Grants Free

    U.S. Environmental Protection Agency grant programs and how to find current notices.

    https://www.epa.gov/grants

  • National Fish and Wildlife Foundation — Apply for a Grant Free

    NFWF’s public apply page and program list.

    https://www.nfwf.org/apply-grant

Oregon / coastal

  • Oregon Coastal Management Program — Coastal Grants Free

    DLCD / OCMP coastal grants, including NOAA-funded habitat protection and restoration.

    https://www.oregon.gov/lcd/ocmp/pages/grants.aspx

  • OWEB — Coastal Wetlands Grants Free

    Coastal wetland acquisition and restoration via OWEB and USFWS.

    https://www.oregon.gov/oweb/grants/pages/coastal-wetlands.aspx

  • Oregon Community Foundation — Grants (North Coast / South Coast) Free

    Same OCF list; use the Region filter for North Coast or South Coast.

    https://oregoncf.org/grants-and-scholarships/grants

  • Oregon Cultural Trust — County and Tribal Cultural Coalitions Free

    Local coalition grant programs in every Oregon county, including the coast.

    https://www.culturaltrust.org/about-us/coalitions/

  • Oregon Arts Commission — Grants Overview Free

    Statewide arts programs open to coastal organizations that meet eligibility.

    https://www.oregon.gov/biz/aboutus/artscommission/grants/Pages/default.aspx

  • Oregon Humanities — Public Program Grants Free

    Statewide humanities grants; coastal nonprofits apply on the same public page.

    https://oregonhumanities.org/programs/grants/public-program-grants-2/

  • Grants.gov — Search Grants Free

    Federal notices that can be filtered by agency and keyword (coastal, NOAA, EPA, and others).

    https://www.grants.gov/search-grants
