Watershed packets only become news in a paper that already exists

Cara Mico · Coast Desk

Correspondence sample. Not a Pioneer article. Not a masthead we own.

Nut

Habitat and sediment studies sit on shelves until someone files a short note the local paper can run. Coast Desk writes that note. Pioneer, Hipfish, Gazette, and Signal stay themselves.

Three numbers a packet can defend

  1. Name the reach (one watershed, one season).
  2. Name the limiting factor the study actually measured.
  3. Name one Monday action a council or county can take without a new website.
