## What we do
**Fieldwork**  
On-the-ground surveys along channels, floodplains, and restoration reaches. Notes that match what is actually there.

**Sampling**  
Sediment, shade, habitat structure, and limiting factors — collected with a method you can cite.

**Stream paths**  
How flow, form, and barriers connect. Maps and narratives that show the path, not just the points.

**Restoration monitoring**  
Before/after and year-to-year checks so a project can prove what changed.
